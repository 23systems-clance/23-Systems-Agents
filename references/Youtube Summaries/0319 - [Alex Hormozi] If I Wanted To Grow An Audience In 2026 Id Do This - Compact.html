If I Wanted To Grow An Audience In 2026, I'd Do This

Alex Hormozi ~53 min March 19, 2026 Watch →
TL;DR

Alex Hormozi breaks down branding as "a deliberate pairing of things through an outcome" and shows how it drives premium pricing (12x), better ad performance (6x CTR), and customer loyalty. He then shares 6 lessons from growing 7.8M followers in 40 months: go all-in on education over entertainment, create content for your ideal customer not the masses, get specific, optimize for revenue not views, favor long-form, and assume nothing about your audience's knowledge.

Key Takeaways
  • 1
    Brand = Deliberate Pairing

    Branding is pairing your product with things your ideal customer likes through positive outcomes. Like Nike pairing with LeBron creates a "winning" association that sells $60 t-shirts.

  • 2
    Good Advertising ≠ Good Branding

    Bud Light/Mulvaney was great advertising (awareness) but bad branding (wrong pairing for their audience). Know the difference.

  • 3
    Entertainment & Education Are Separate Pools

    Entertainment viewers don't convert to buyers. Education viewers buy books, join lists, apply to work with you. They're separate audiences, not a funnel.

  • 4
    Optimize for Revenue, Not Views

    All views aren't equal. Education content drives less views but far more revenue per viewer.

  • 5
    Clear Beats Clever

    Vague thumbnails/titles hurt. Be crystal clear about what your content delivers — people click on what they understand.

  • 6
    Lead with Proof, Not Confirmation

    For educational content, the intro should establish credibility ("here's why you should listen to me") not just confirm the headline.

  • 7
    Brand Is a Bouquet

    Think of brand elements as flowers — curate deliberately, pull weeds (bad pairings), and don't scatter randomly.
